### Identified Research Gap:
The research papers on time series forecasting with LSTM networks collectively share the limitation of focusing on specific modeling techniques (such as deep Poisson mixtures, LLMs, and Gaussian Processes) without addressing the broader challenges of interpretability and explainability in time series forecasting. They miss the opportunity to explore hybrid approaches that combine the strengths of different modeling techniques to enhance forecasting accuracy and robustness. Additionally, the technical challenge of handling real-world time series data with high dimensionality, noise, and missing values remains unaddressed in these papers.
